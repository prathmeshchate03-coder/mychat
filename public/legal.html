About, privacy and terms

About JenChat

JenChat is a free guest chat site made by Bittu and DCD. You can talk in public rooms or create private rooms without signing up.

Privacy

  • We do not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your nickname is saved only in your own browser.
  • The last 50 messages of each room are kept in server memory and are deleted when the room is empty or the server restarts. We do not keep a permanent archive.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to limit spam. Our hosting provider and server logs may also record IP addresses.
  • The IP address of recent messages is kept in server memory so that moderators can act on reports. When you press report on a message, its text, nickname, room and sender IP are kept for review (see below), and its text, nickname and room are also written to our logs.
  • If we ban an IP address or close a room, that decision is saved in a database until the ban ends or a moderator removes it. Reports, including the reported message and sender IP, are saved there for up to 14 days. We do not store normal chat messages in the database.
  • Your browser stores your nickname, a random ID that makes the block feature work, and your block list. People who create a private room also get a secret code stored in their browser, which proves they are the owner. None of this is sent anywhere except to our server when you use the chat.
  • If a private room owner removes you, your IP address is remembered in server memory for one hour so that you cannot rejoin that room.
  • Private messages (DMs) are text only and go from one person to another while both are online. They are not saved in the database and are not shown on the site. So that moderators can check a report, a copy of each recent private message, together with the sender's IP address, is kept in server memory for up to 12 hours. If you report a private message, its text, the sender's nickname and IP address are kept for review as described above. Your browser also stores a secret code that proves who you are for private messages, and the list of people whose messages you blocked.
  • Accounts are optional. If you create one we store your email address, username and a hashed password (never the password itself). Verification and password-reset mails are sent through a Google (Gmail) mail relay. To delete your account, email us.
  • Pictures can be sent only by verified accounts. A picture is stored on our server for 7 days and then deleted automatically; the sender can delete it earlier. Camera and location data (EXIF) is removed. Anyone who already saved a copy keeps it, deleting cannot undo that. The uploader's account name and IP address are recorded with the picture. If a picture is reported, a copy is kept for moderators for 14 days even if the sender deletes it. Pictures sent in private messages also pass through our server.
  • The owner of a private room can write a topic, description and rules for it, pin or delete messages, and ban people from the room. These room settings are kept for up to 30 days after the last change. If you are banned from a room, your IP address (and your account, if you were logged in) is saved with that room's settings until the owner removes the ban or the settings expire.
  • Do not share personal information in chat. Other users can read everything you post in a room.

Terms of use

  • You must be 18 or older to use JenChat.
  • Do not post illegal content, threats, hate, harassment, sexual content involving minors, sexual, violent or non-consensual pictures, spam or scams.
  • We may remove messages, block users or close rooms at any time without notice.
  • You are responsible for what you write. JenChat is provided as it is, without any guarantee.
