About JenChat

JenChat is a free guest chat site made by Bittu and DCD. You can talk in public rooms or create private rooms without signing up.

Privacy

  • We do not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your nickname is saved only in your own browser.
  • The last 50 messages of each room are kept in server memory and are deleted when the room is empty or the server restarts. We do not keep a permanent archive.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to limit spam. Our hosting provider and server logs may also record IP addresses.
  • When you press report on a message, its text, nickname and room are written to our logs for review.
  • Do not share personal information in chat. Other users can read everything you post in a room.

Terms of use

  • You must be 18 or older to use JenChat.
  • Do not post illegal content, threats, hate, harassment, sexual content involving minors, spam or scams.
  • We may remove messages, block users or close rooms at any time without notice.
  • You are responsible for what you write. JenChat is provided as it is, without any guarantee.
